How to revise effectively

  1. Read one topic and explain the key idea in your own words.
  2. Answer a few questions without looking at the explanation.
  3. Check mistakes and identify the idea you missed.
  4. Return to that topic another day and try a fresh example.

These guides are original introductory explanations and do not replace school materials or official textbooks.
