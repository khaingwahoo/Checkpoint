ABOUT EDUBRIDGE

One place to practise. One clear path to improve.

EduBridge is an independent learning platform built around a simple improvement loop: practise, identify weak topics, correct mistakes and see progress over time.

Learning paths

Stage 7 Science is the first live school programme. EduBridge is structured to expand into Primary Stage 6, Lower Secondary Stages 8–9, IGCSE, EduBridge Medical and, later, Myanmar Curriculum without creating separate learning systems for each area.

What makes EduBridge different

  • Topic-based original exam-style practice.
  • A Mistake Book that turns wrong answers into recovery practice.
  • Skill Map and EduBridge Readiness indicators based on real activity.
  • Daily Challenge and study reports that make progress visible.
  • An expert review process designed for high-quality, carefully maintained content.
Independent platform: EduBridge is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of Cambridge International Education. Curriculum references are used descriptively to explain learning alignment.

FOR PARENTS

See the learning behind the score.

EduBridge study reports are designed to show more than a quiz percentage. A completed session can include study time, score, corrections reviewed, topic mastery change and mistakes fixed.

Study report example

Verified study reports

EduBridge can attach a student name and report ID to a study record so a forwarded report still identifies the original learner. In this prototype the record is stored locally in the browser; cross-device verification will require the account/database system planned for the production version.
