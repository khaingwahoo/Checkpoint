Legal and privacy

Privacy policy

Pre-launch version — 5 November 2026. This page describes the downloadable website prototype. Before public launch, this policy must be checked against the actual deployed code, hosting, sign-up and advertising services.

Information handled by the prototype

The prototype may ask for a name, email address and learning or class details. It stores certain account profiles, quiz attempts and progress information in the visitor’s browser using local storage. The prototype does not provide a production-grade online account service; clearing browser data can remove local progress. Do not reuse important passwords on this demo.

Third-party resources

External resources such as Google Fonts may receive technical information, including an IP address, when requested by the browser. Hosting providers may separately process routine technical request information under their own terms.

Advertising and cookies

No Google AdSense ad code is installed in this pre-launch package. If advertising or analytics is introduced, this policy must be updated to explain the providers, purposes, applicable cookie and consent choices, and how users may manage them.

Children and family use

The platform may be used by younger learners. No verified age or parental-consent system is included in this prototype. Appropriate compliance measures must be implemented before enabling child-directed features that require them or advertising that would not be suitable for minors.

Your choices

You can clear locally saved prototype data through browser settings. For an operational service, the operator must provide a verified method for requesting access, correction, deletion, and support, as applicable.

Contact details

A public support contact has not yet been configured. The operator must publish a working contact method before launching account registration or monetized services.
